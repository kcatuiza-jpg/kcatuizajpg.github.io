Coffee with a conscience

At Barako Bro's, we believe great coffee starts with great relationships. We source directly from smallholder farms in Benguet, Batangas, and Mindanao — paying above fair-trade prices.

Every batch is roasted right in our shop, ensuring peak freshness. Our baristas are craftspeople who love to talk about tasting notes, brew methods, and the perfect crema.
